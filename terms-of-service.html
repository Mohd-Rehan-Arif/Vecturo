Section 5: Ethics & Best Practices

5 Local SEO Compliance Standards

Vecturo is firmly committed to ethical, transparent, white-hat digital engineering. All recommendations, codebases, and local optimizations deployed by Vecturo strictly adhere to the following standards:

Authentic Business Data (NAP)

We only optimize verified, legitimate physical storefront locations and service territories. We do not create artificial shell locations or virtual forwarding addresses.

Zero Spam & No Doorway Pages

We adhere strictly to Google Webmaster Guidelines. We reject keyword stuffing, hidden text, automated citation spam, and manipulative cloaking.

We advocate for genuine customer feedback. We strictly prohibit fake review generation, bot review farms, and deceptive review gating techniques.
