Legal & Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

Core Privacy Principle: Simple Business Data Only

Vecturo does NOT collect, store, or process any sensitive personal financial data, banking credentials, credit/debit card numbers, private account passwords, or government identification numbers.

We exist solely to assist local brick-and-mortar storefronts in establishing their public digital presence. The only data we request through our diagnostic tools or contact forms are basic, public-facing business details (such as your business name, city, service type, and business contact email/phone) provided voluntarily to prepare your digital audit or book a discovery session.

1 Information We Collect

When you interact with Vecturo (through our website at vecturo.in, our diagnostic tools, or our scheduling forms), we collect only simple business and contact details:

  • Storefront & Business Information: Business or trade name, physical city or target municipality, and primary industry category.
  • Representative Contact Information: Full name of the business owner or authorized contact person, business email address, and direct phone number.
  • Public Digital Presence Data: Public website URL (if one exists), whether your Google Business Profile is claimed, and self-reported business goals or questions.
  • Technical Diagnostics (Anonymous): Standard web server logs including browser type, referring URL, and basic device metrics to ensure fast page load speeds.

Explicit Exclusion: Under no circumstance will Vecturo ask you for banking login credentials, bank routing codes, payment card PINs, or private consumer records.

2 How We Use Your Simple Business Data

We use the limited information provided solely for legitimate business enablement purposes:

  • To perform public local search audits and generate customized 8-page competitor footprint reports.
  • To schedule, confirm, and host your 20-minute video discovery session.
  • To send technical recommendations and report deliverables to your nominated email (vecturosupport@gmail.com).
  • To respond directly to inquiries and provide ongoing customer support.

3 Zero Third-Party Data Selling

Vecturo does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your business contact information with data brokers, advertising aggregators, or external marketing entities. Your details remain strictly between your business and Vecturo.

4 Data Storage & Security Measures

All communications and diagnostic requests transmitted through our website utilize industry-standard 256-bit SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption in transit. While no digital pipeline is completely impenetrable, we implement stringent administrative and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of your simple business details.

5 Your Rights & Data Removal Requests

You have full control over any information you submit. At any time, you may request:

  • A summary of what business contact information we have on file for your storefront.
  • Immediate deletion or purging of your submitted business data from our records.
  • Opt-out from any future diagnostic follow-up communications.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at vecturosupport@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Removal Request".
